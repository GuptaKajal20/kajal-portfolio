01 / 05Case study overview

QikD connects people with verified agents who help them get government and business documents, like PAN cards and voter IDs. Feedback showed that first-time users didn’t understand how the service worked, so many never started. In my first product role, I worked with the Co-founder on three things: making sense of user feedback, making the process clear on the website, and following up with people who showed interest.

05 / 0504 · What I took away

What did my first product role teach me?

For a service people use rarely, clarity is the product.

Scroll to flip ↻
05 / 05
What I did

Turned scattered reviews into a clear list of recurring issues, helped make the process visible on the website, and helped bring back interested users through follow-up emails.

Learning

For a service people use rarely, clarity is the product. If someone can’t see what happens after they pay, they don’t start.

Next time

I’d save my work as I go. I didn’t keep wireframes or documents from this internship, and I’d set up a measure of how many first-time visitors start an application.
