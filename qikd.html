QikD: making a service platform easy to understand

QikD connects people with verified agents for documents like PAN cards. First-time users didn't understand how to use it.

RoleProduct Manager Intern
TimelineMay – Jul 2025
Worked withCo-founder
FocusFeedback, website structure, emails
01Problem

First-time users couldn't see how the service worked.

  • Which service to pick, what happens after paying, and how they'd get updates were all unclear.
02Discovery

Reviews and feedback pointed to the same gaps.

  • Users didn't understand the process before paying.
  • The logged-in experience needed work.
  • Some interested people asked questions and never got a reply.
03Approach

Organised all feedback, then prioritised with the Co-founder.

  • Put reviews and feedback in a spreadsheet and grouped them into recurring issues.
  • Three key problems: unclear process, weak logged-in experience, no follow-up.
04Solution

Make every step visible, and follow up.

  • Restructured the website and landing page into clear steps: Select service → Secure payment → Agent assigned → Processing → Delivered.
  • Email follow-ups and reminders to bring back interested users.
Lesson

For a service people use rarely, clarity is the product. If someone can't see what happens after they pay, they don't start.
