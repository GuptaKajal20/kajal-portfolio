QikD: making a service platform easy to understand

QikD connects people with verified agents for government and business documents like PAN cards and voter IDs. Feedback showed that first-time users didn't understand how to use it.

RoleProduct Manager Intern
TimelineMay – Jul 2025
Worked withCo-founder
FocusFeedback, website structure, emails

01Listening to feedback

I went through user reviews and feedback and organised them in a spreadsheet to find recurring issues, prioritise them with the Co-founder, and help prepare responses.

Observed

Users didn't understand the platform

First-time users struggled to see how to use QikD: which service to pick, what happens after paying, and how they'd get updates.

Observed

The logged-in experience needed work

Once inside, the experience also needed improvement.

02A clearer website

I contributed to improving the website's information architecture and landing page, so users can understand the platform and find services more easily. The landing page now walks users through the process step by step.

Select service
Secure payment
Agent assigned
Processing
Document delivered

03Re-engaging interested users

Some people showed interest or asked questions but never got a reply. I helped with email campaigns to bring them back, with follow-ups and reminders such as passport-related updates, and I monitored email opens. The response was positive. I don't have verified numbers, so I don't show any.

04What I learned

This was my first product role. The lesson that stuck: for a service people use rarely, clarity is the product. If someone can't see what happens after they pay, they don't start. I'd also now save my work as I go, because I didn't keep wireframes or documents from this internship.
