SocialAddressGrowth & onboarding

Launch was day one. Then I tracked where users quit, fixed the onboarding and wrote 11 blogs.

SocialAddress does a lot in one place. That only helps if users find the features.

RoleProduct Management Intern
TimelineJan – Aug 2026
ToolsGoogle Analytics, user calls
OutputTooltips, product tours, 11 blogs
01Problem

Features shipped, but users didn't find them.

  • Link-in-bio, Shop, Booking, auto-DMs and the SAM AI assistant all live in one product.
  • Value depended on users discovering features and finishing set-up.
02Discovery

Users dropped off in key flows and missed whole features.

  • Checked Google Analytics event tracking, then studied where users came from and went next.
  • Found drop-offs in sign-up and store creation, and features users never found.
  • User calls showed where the product felt confusing.
03Approach

Matched where users quit with why they quit.

  • Combined drop-off points from analytics with what users said on calls.
  • Grouped the issues into two: getting stuck inside a flow and never discovering a feature.
04Solution

Guide users inside the product, and reach them outside it.

  • Inside: product tours, tooltips and onboarding improvements, and underused features made more visible.
  • Outside: 11 blogs published under my name, each starting from a problem merchants described.
Next

Measure each change against the drop-off it targeted, for example store-creation completion before and after the tooltips.
