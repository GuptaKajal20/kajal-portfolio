Booking: less admin around every appointment

Booking a slot was easy. Collecting information before it, and handling changes after it, wasn't.

RoleProduct Management Intern
OwnershipIndependent: research, roadmap, PRDs, delivery
Research11 doctor interviews + workshop sellers
Worked withDesign and Engineering
01Problem

The admin around each booking happened off-platform.

  • Medical history and reports were collected separately, usually on WhatsApp.
  • Reschedules, delays and timing questions were handled with calls.
02Discovery

Owners whose time is the product described the same manual work.

  • 11 doctor interviews, plus workshop and course sellers from my merchant research.
  • Meeting links shared by hand, enrolment run on forms and spreadsheets, customers dropping off when switching platforms.
  • Counter-signal: some local clinics don't need online booking, and some hosts need a conversation before payment.
03Approach

Grouped the recurring problems and prioritised them on a roadmap.

  • Pulled recurring problems out of the interview notes: collecting information, changing plans and trust.
  • Set the goal: reduce the admin around a booking, not replace the conversation.
  • Wrote the PRDs and worked with design and engineering through delivery.
04Solution

Bring the before and after into the product.

  • Booking forms with document upload, set by each business. Live
  • Booking → calendar, with reschedules and notifications to both sides. Live
  • Branded booking pages, because trust kept coming up. Live
  • SAM AI scheduling: the AI proposes, the owner confirms, since owners avoided letting AI act for them. In testing
Next

Measure the admin it removes: no-show rate, reschedule rate and how many bookings arrive with complete information.
