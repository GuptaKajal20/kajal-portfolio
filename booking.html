Booking: less admin around every appointment

Booking a slot is only half the job. Before a session, owners need information from the customer, and after booking, plans change. I owned the improvements that brought both into the product.

RoleProduct Management Intern
OwnershipIndependent: research, roadmap, PRDs, delivery
Research11 doctor interviews + workshop sellers
Worked withDesign and Engineering

01Context

SocialAddress Booking lets any business share one link where customers book an appointment, a 1:1 session, an interview or a workshop ticket. It serves businesses whose time is the product: doctors and clinics, workshop hosts, coaches and consultants. This was separate from Shop, my main project.

02What users described

My main research was 11 interviews with doctors. Workshop and course sellers from my merchant interviews described the same manual work.

What they describedWho
Information needed before the session (history, reports) is collected separately, usually on WhatsAppDoctors, clinics
Enrolment runs on Google Forms, payment screenshots, pre-written messages and spreadsheetsWorkshop and course hosts
Meeting links are created and shared by handClinics, workshop hosts
Plans change: no-shows, late arrivals, delays, and repeated calls about timingsDoctors, clinics
Customers drop off when they have to switch between platformsClinic
Counter-signal

Some doctors don't need online booking at all, because their patients are local and prefer to call. One workshop host found forms alone didn't convert, because people wanted a conversation before paying. So the goal was to reduce the admin around a booking, not replace the conversation.

03What changed

Live

Booking forms with document upload

Each business chooses what to ask before a session, and customers can upload documents while booking. For example: a doctor asks for medical history and reports, a workshop host asks for skill level, and a consultant asks what to discuss.

Live

One connected booking → calendar flow

A confirmed booking appears in the calendar automatically. From there the owner can reschedule, and both sides get notified whenever a slot changes, with no calls needed.

Live

Branded booking pages

Owners match the page to their brand colours, so the link feels like their business. Trust came up again and again in the research.

In testing

SAM AI scheduling

The owner tells SAM what they want in plain words. SAM proposes the calendar change, and the owner reviews and confirms it.

Key decision

The AI proposes, and the owner confirms. In my merchant research, small-business owners avoided letting AI act for them. A confirmation step makes AI useful without taking away control.

04My contribution

  • Interviewed 11 doctors and pulled out the recurring problems
  • Built the roadmap and prioritised the improvements
  • Wrote the PRDs
  • Worked with design and engineering through delivery

05What I'd do next

  • Measure the admin it removes: no-show rate, reschedule rate, and how many bookings arrive with complete information.
  • Research beyond doctors: interview coaches and consultants directly, since the forms are designed for them too.
