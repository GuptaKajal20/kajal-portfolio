Collab Pro: brand collaborations and a portfolio, in one place

A free platform for Indian creators. It helps them find brand collaborations, build a portfolio they can share with brands, and see who viewed it.

RoleSolo: product, design decisions, build
Built withAI coding tools (Claude Code)
StatusLive; no user feedback yet
LinkOpen Collab Pro ↗

01The problem

Brand collaboration openings for creators are scattered across posts, websites and announcements. And when a creator does pitch a brand, they often send a folder link or a list of profiles rather than one clear portfolio.

I'd seen this side of the creator economy while working on creator retention at Nurdd. Collab Pro was my way to take one problem from idea to a live product myself.

02What it does

Find collabs

Fresh brand-collaboration listings, collected automatically every morning. Creators apply at the original source.

Build a portfolio

A drag-and-drop portfolio builder with ready-made templates, shared with brands as one public link.

See who's interested

Portfolio analytics: views, visits and clicks on the creator's dashboard.

Spot trends

A Trend Radar that shows what's trending on YouTube and Instagram.

03Product decisions

  • Free for creators. Early creators earn little, and paid tools are a barrier. I heard the same about paid tools in my SocialAddress research.
  • Apply at the source. Collab Pro points creators to the original listing instead of acting as a middleman.
  • One link to share. A portfolio replaces a folder of screenshots and profile links.

04What's next

Collab Pro is live, but no creators have tested it yet. My next step is to put it in front of 5 creators, watch them build a portfolio, and find out whether the listings are useful enough to bring them back.
