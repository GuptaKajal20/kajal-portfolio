Creator Dashboard: giving creators a reason to come back

Creators joined Nurdd but didn't come back. Two pieces of work: a dashboard to bring them back, and free tools to bring new people in.

RoleProduct Intern
TimelineJul – Nov 2025
Worked withCo-founder, Designer
StatusFigma design; launch not confirmed

Creator Dashboard

01Problem

Creators joined, then stopped using it.

  • Nurdd connects creators and brands. Brands come for active creators, so retention was the core problem.
  • Creators saw their numbers but didn't know what to do with them.
02Discovery

Analytics alone don't form a habit.

  • Researched competing creator platforms: how creators and agencies use them, judge performance and get hired.
03Approach

Turned the research into the three questions a creator asks.

  • Am I getting closer to my goal? · What should I do next? · What's working right now?
  • One tab per question, designed with the Co-founder and Designer.
04Solution

Pair every insight with an action.

  • My Journey: progress toward a goal.
  • Action Plan: action cards, each opening an AI helper.
  • Insights Hub: daily performance, a reason to check daily.

Free creator tools

01Problem

Judging creator performance by hand is hard.

  • Is this engagement rate good? Is this audience real? What's a fair rate?
02Discovery

Creators and brands ask the same few questions before trusting a platform.

  • These questions came up across the creator-platform research.
03Approach

One question, one tool.

  • Each tool takes a few inputs and returns an instant result against benchmarks.
04Solution

Free calculators on the public website, with no sign-up.

  • People get value before joining, which builds trust and brings them to Nurdd.
  • I contributed to these tools; the team has updated them since. See them ↗
Design only

The dashboard is a Figma design and its launch isn't confirmed. Next time I'd test it with 5–6 creators before polishing, and track weekly returning creators.
