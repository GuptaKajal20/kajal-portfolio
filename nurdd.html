Creator Dashboard: giving creators a reason to come back

Creators joined Nurdd but didn't use it regularly. The goal was to make the platform part of their routine: show their progress, what to work on next, and the steps to get there.

RoleProduct Intern
TimelineJul – Nov 2025
Worked withCo-founder, Designer
StatusFigma design; launch not confirmed

01The problem

Nurdd connects creators and brands. Brands come for active creators, and creators who don't come back get no value or collaborations. Retention was the core problem.

I researched competing creator platforms: how creators and agencies use them, how they judge performance, and how creators get hired. Then I worked with the Co-founder and Designer to turn these ideas into designs.

02The dashboard: one question per tab

TabCreator's questionWhy it brings them back
My Journey"Am I getting closer to my goal?"Progress toward a goal, follower growth, posting consistency and monthly wins. Visible progress keeps creators motivated.
Action Plan"What should I do next?"Action cards (trends, Reels, hashtags, posting times, comment replies), each opening an AI helper. Data becomes a next step.
Insights Hub"What's working right now?"Today's performance, audience, saves, profile views and content types. It changes daily, so there's a reason to check daily.
Key decision

Don't just show analytics. Pair every insight with an action. Analytics tell creators what happened. A habit forms when the product also tells them what to do next.

03Free creator tools

Creators and brands find it hard to judge performance by hand. Is this engagement rate good? Is this audience real? What's a fair rate? Nurdd's free tools answer these from a few inputs, with an instant result against benchmarks and no sign-up.

EnterFollowers, likes, comments
GetEngagement rate + tier
CompareBenchmark verdict

Example: the Engagement Rate Calculator on nurdd.club/tools ↗

Why it matters for the business: creators and brands get value before joining, which builds trust and brings people to Nurdd.

My part: I contributed to Nurdd's free creator tools on the public website. The Nurdd team has updated them since.

04What I'd do differently

  • Test before polishing: show the dashboard to 5–6 creators to learn which tab they'd really open every day.
  • Define success upfront: for example, weekly returning creators, to know whether the design changes behaviour.
