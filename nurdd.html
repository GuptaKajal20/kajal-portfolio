01 / 06Case study overview

Nurdd connects creators with brands. Brands look for active creators, so when creators joined and then stopped using Nurdd, they missed out on collaborations and the platform had less to offer brands. Working with the Co-founder and the Designer, I helped design a dashboard that gives creators a reason to come back every day.

02 / 0601 · Why creators left

Why weren’t creators coming back?

They could see their numbers, but not what to do with them.

Scroll to flip ↻
02 / 06

Retention was the problem the team was focused on. A creator would join, look at their numbers, and then have nothing telling them what to do next. Without a reason to return, they drifted away, and fewer active creators meant less for brands to choose from.

The loop we wanted to break

JoinsSigns up to Nurdd
Sees numbersFollowers, likes, views
No next step“So what do I do now?”
Stops returningNurdd isn’t part of the routine
Fewer active creatorsLess value for brands

The goalMake Nurdd part of a creator’s routine by showing their progress, what to work on next, and the steps to get there.

05 / 0604 · The design

What did the dashboard look like?

Three tabs, designed in Figma with the Co-founder and Designer.

Scroll to flip ↻
05 / 06

I turned the research into the dashboard’s structure, and worked with the Co-founder and the Designer to turn these ideas into Figma designs.

Figma design. Sample data only: “Priya” and all numbers are placeholders. Not confirmed as launched.

How the tabs work together

In the designs, Insights Hub shows that Reels drove most of today’s engagement. Action Plan turns that into a step, “Add more Reels & Videos”, with an AI helper that suggests Reel scripts. The insight and the action sit side by side, so the creator never has to work out what to do next.

06 / 0605 · Looking back

What happened, and what would I do next?

A design built around one idea: give creators a next step.

Scroll to flip ↻
06 / 06
Outcome

A Figma design for a three-tab creator dashboard, made with the Co-founder and the Designer. I don’t know whether it launched after my internship, so I present it as a design.

Learning

Retention comes from giving users a next step, not more data.

What I’d do next

Show the dashboard to 5–6 creators before polishing it, to learn which tab they’d really open every day, and track weekly returning creators to see if it changes behaviour.
