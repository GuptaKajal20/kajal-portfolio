01 / 04Case study overview

Creators and brands often can’t tell whether an account is actually doing well. Nurdd’s free creator tools answer that from a few numbers, with an instant result and no sign-up. That gives people real value before they join, which builds trust and brings them to Nurdd. I contributed to these tools on Nurdd’s public website.

What were creators and brands trying to figure out?

Whether an account was actually doing well.

Scroll to flip ↻
02 / 04

Creators and brands both look at the same numbers, followers, likes and comments, but they rarely know what “good” looks like. Working it out by hand means guessing, with nothing to compare against.

The questions people kept trying to answer

Is this engagement rate good?Is this audience real?What’s a fair rate for a collaboration?

The gapWithout a benchmark, creators can’t tell how they’re doing, and brands can’t tell who to work with.
